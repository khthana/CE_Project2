ABSTRACT

 

                Infrastructure of the Helicopter with Automatic Control Device is to do for development the control board with equipment and the program to control blades of helicopter, it can still handle on the air. The aim of the final research is to do the Helicopter with Automatic Control Device.

                The control board use sensors and Fuzzy Logic rules to control the microcontroller make servo motors’ pulse for adjust blade of helicopter. User can watch all status by the monitor.
