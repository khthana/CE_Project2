Teerapong Mhodhiran
Apinetr Aunagul Advisor
ABSTRACT

Internet auction can make use of technologies combine with the strength of ordinary auction to enhance business opportunity in electronic commerce. Buyers post their needs on the web site so that sellers can propose the deal that match buyer's needs. In this way, buyers can get what they need while sellers can expand their target group.

This thesis concerns with theories in constructing internet auction system and the analysis and design of the system. Users must be registered member in order to use the services through web browser. The system's tasks are handling data and process in the internet auction including maintenance work.
