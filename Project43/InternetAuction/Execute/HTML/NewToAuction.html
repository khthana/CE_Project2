Learn to use Internet Auction

First.. You will need to register as an Internet Auction member if you haven't done so already. It's free and only takes a couple of minutes.. you will immediately get your username and password .. (Please remember your password right away). Before you use this Internet Auction as buyer or seller , you must login to join our system.

Buyer

1. Choose the menu that you want from the buyer menu list. If you are general user who don't know much about computer specification.. try General Requirement .. our system will match proper computer set for your requirement.

2. Try Add Technical Specification for give us your requirement in technical term. Sellers will offer their computer specification and total price for bidding.

 Seller

1. Choose Add Specification for give us your specification of computer set that you can serve to buyer who will give general requirement. System will match this computer set for proper buyer requirement.

2.Bidding is the menu for bid in each auction project. You have to offer your computer specification which proper for buyer technical specification project that you want.

How we get Winner ?

1. For "General Requirement" , the winner is the seller who has ever add his specification of the computer set and these specification match for buyer requirement. The winner can be more than one seller, so we will send all of computer set specification (all of winner) to buyer. The buyer can decide to choose which specification is proper by himself and buy it , or he can open new auction project in Technical Requirement.

2. For "Add Technical Requirement" , we will find the seller whom his total price of computer specification is minimum (compare with other sellers in the same auction project).
