ABSTRACT

                Computer engineering department of King Mongkut’s Institute Technology of Ladkrabang. That researches computer technology and have many people interacted with. WebPages people can retrieve the information their prefer, So Computer department has WebPages that provide many information such as course list of lecturer and learner announcement and education link.

                This era, Information technology has worldwide use and usually presents in multimedia form. Then, development of computer department presentation should in this way. This presentation technology can attract users with interactive and animation. So this project use multimedia technology base on subject that had learn to construct a presentation of department using WWW  

This project’s report composes of system requirements and system design by use basis of Object- oriented. This report is used for develop introduction to Computer Engineering Department web-base and stand alone system by use computer multimedia technology in presentation. This presentation technology can attract users and make it easy to understand for beginners what this system present.
