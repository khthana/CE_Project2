ABSTRACT

                 Nowadays face recognition is researched extensively. There are many researches about Face Recognition is developed to use in any application. Increasing ability of computer, computer vision, is very interesting and very profitable.
                Doorman Robot is a robot having ability to recognize frontal face of people is incoming a building. Then, the robot verifies that facial face with any faces that have been kept in the database. Several techniques about image processing are used Such as, Filter, Labeling etc. The extracted feature from facial face, eye, nose, mouth, is used for recognize. Coefficient Correlation is an approach that is selected for matching. The Doorman Robot may be used instead of guards or be trend to develop to several related application.
